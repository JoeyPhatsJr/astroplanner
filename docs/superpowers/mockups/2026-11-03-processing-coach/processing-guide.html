What stands out

Before 3:30 AM, about half the frames were lost
From 3:30 on, 91% made it into the stack. Before that, 51%. The cut was mostly from stretched stars (roundness under 0.74).
164 frames couldn't be aligned at all
Siril recorded no stars for them. With only ~44 stars per frame to start with, a little cloud or dew is enough. The app would check each frame's sky brightness to tell which it was.
No dark or flat frames in the folder
So the guide skips those steps and tells you what that costs (and how to shoot flats next time, if you want).

Your recipe for this night

  1. ✓
    Convert & debayer
    Colour pattern GRBG (from the frame headers)
    The S30 saves colour as a checkerboard of red, green and blue pixels. Siril has to know the pattern to rebuild colour.
  2. ✓
    Align the frames
    Two-pass registration
    Your frames have ~44 stars each, which is few. Two passes pick the best reference frame first, so fewer frames fail.
  3. 3
    Choose which frames to keep
    Drop frames with roundness under 0.74 → 654 frames, 1 h 49 m
    Trade-off: more frames = smoother background; rounder stars = sharper picture. Your stretched frames are mostly before 3:30 AM.
    seqapplyreg pp_light -filter-round=0.74 # example syntax
  4. 4
    Stack
    Winsorized sigma clipping, 3 / 3
    With 654 frames, this reliably removes satellite trails and hot pixels without throwing away real signal.
